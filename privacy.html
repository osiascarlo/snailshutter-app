01

Commitment to Data Privacy (RA 10173)

SnailShutter Photography Studio (referred to as "SnailShutter", "the Studio", "We", "Us", or "Our"), operating at EJR Business Center 2, Poblacion, Alaminos City, Pangasinan, is dedicated to upholding your fundamental right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, record, organize, store, update, use, consolidate, block, or erase your Personal Information in adherence to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and the regulatory advisories issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

Privacy-by-Design in Mindful Photography: We only collect information that is strictly necessary to fulfill your studio reservations, facilitate client communication, deliver high-resolution retouched media, and maintain platform security. We never sell or rent your personal information to third-party data brokers.

Lawful Basis & Purpose of Processing

Under Section 12 of the Data Privacy Act of 2012, SnailShutter processes your personal data on the lawful bases of contractual necessity, consent, and legitimate interest for the following purposes:

  • Service Execution: To schedule studio space, assign photographers, configure studio backdrops, and complete your mindful photography session.
  • Identity & Account Verification: To dispatch 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) verification emails via automated SMTP systems to verify email authenticity upon registration.
  • Communication & Status Updates: To send automated transaction alerts, downpayment verification notices, upcoming appointment reminders, and gallery readiness alerts.
  • Digital Gallery Delivery: To generate personalized, authenticated client galleries where you can view proofs and download full-resolution finished deliverables.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent bookings, spam abuse, and unauthorized system access.

07

Data Security, Encryption & Safeguards

SnailShutter implements multi-layered physical, technical, and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS): All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern 256-bit TLS encryption.
  • Password Hashing: User passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard bcrypt with multiple salt rounds before database insertion.
  • Brute-Force & Lockout Protection: Our authentication systems monitor failed login attempts. Accounts experiencing repeated incorrect credentials trigger temporary cooldown lockout alerts to prevent credential stuffing.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Studio staff accounts are segmented into client, photographer/staff, and administrator privilege tiers. Staff members only access customer data required to fulfill assigned photoshoots.
08

Retention & File Archival Policy

We maintain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by Philippine laws:

  • User Account & Booking Histories: Maintained for the lifetime of your active client account or for five (5) years following your last booking to comply with Philippine tax and accounting auditing standards.
  • Finished Client Media: High-resolution delivered images remain stored in your client gallery portal for a minimum of 60 days after upload. After 60 days, SnailShutter may archive or purge online files to free storage capacity. Clients are strongly encouraged to download their completed images upon delivery.
  • Payment Receipts: Uploaded transaction proof screenshots are retained for up to two (2) years for financial reconciliation, after which they are securely deleted.
09

Your Statutory Rights Under RA 10173

As a Data Subject under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you possess clear, enforceable rights regarding your personal information:

Your Rights as a Data Subject:
  • Right to be Informed: To know whether your personal data is being processed, and the details of such processing.
  • Right to Access: To obtain a copy of your personal data maintained in our systems.
  • Right to Rectification: To dispute inaccuracies and request prompt correction of outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request suspension, withdrawal, or deletion of your personal data upon reasonable grounds.
  • Right to Damages: To be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, or unlawful processing.
  • Right to Data Portability: To obtain an electronic copy of your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Right to File a Complaint: To lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at privacy.gov.ph if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a written request to our Data Protection Officer at snailshutterstudio@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within fifteen (15) business days.

10

Cookies & Session Management

Our web portal utilizes lightweight session cookies and local storage tokens strictly necessary for the technical operation of the website:

  • Authentication Session Cookies: Encrypted cookies (e.g. connect.sid) required to keep you signed in while browsing through your dashboard and booking steps.
  • Security Flags: Session cookies are transmitted with SameSite=Lax and HttpOnly/Secure parameters to guard against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) and Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
  • No Invasive Ad Trackers: SnailShutter does not run invasive cross-site advertising tracking cookies or sell your browsing history to third-party ad networks.
11

Protection of Children & Minors

SnailShutter offers family, birthday, baptismal, and graduation photography packages featuring children and infants:

  • Our online booking platform is intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years of age or emancipated minors under Philippine law.
  • Bookings for minors must be registered, scheduled, and authorized by a parent or legal guardian.
  • A parent or legal guardian must accompany minors during all photography sessions at our studio premises.
  • Photographs of minors are never published on public studio social media channels or portfolios without express, written affirmative consent from the parent or legal guardian.
