All wireframe SVGs are crafted using standard SVG vector primitives (<rect>, <line>, <text>, <circle>) without raster images, external font dependencies, or complex clip paths. This guarantees 100% fidelity when importing into Microsoft Word:

  • Method 1 (Recommended): Download appendix-h-full-wireframes.docx directly. It comes pre-formatted in standard Letter Portrait (8.5" × 11") with 0.5" academic margins, containing all 4 sheets.
  • Method 2 (Clipboard Paste): Click "Copy Current Sheet (Word)", open Microsoft Word, and press Ctrl + V.
