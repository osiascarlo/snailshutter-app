Session Booking & 10% Downpayment Policy

To secure a reserved date, studio space, and dedicated equipment/photographer time, all appointments booked via our web portal require a mandatory 10% Downpayment.

Strict Non-Refundable Downpayment Policy: The 10% downpayment is strictly non-refundable. When you book a time slot, that slot is immediately locked and unavailable to any other client. The downpayment covers administrative booking costs, schedule holding, and studio preparation.

Payment Verification Process:

  1. Clients must scan the official Studio GCash QR code or transfer to the designated GCash mobile number (0912 345 6789 · SnailShutter Studio) for the exact 10% downpayment amount displayed during checkout.
  2. A clear screenshot of the successful transaction confirmation (displaying the reference number, amount, date, and time) must be uploaded via the booking portal.
  3. Our staff verifies the payment against incoming merchant records. Bookings remain in "Pending Verification" status until verified, upon which you will receive an automated confirmation email.
  4. Remaining Balance: The remaining 90% balance of the package and any applied add-ons is payable at the studio upon conclusion of the photography session via Cash or GCash.

Operating Hours & Lunch Break Policy

SnailShutter operates Monday through Sunday, from 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Philippine Standard Time, Asia/Manila), open 7 days a week. Select official national holidays may have adjusted schedules as announced by studio management.

Studio Lunch Break Interval (12:01 PM – 12:59 PM): The studio observes a daily rest and recharge interval for equipment cooling, sanitization, and staff lunch break between 12:01 PM and 12:59 PM. If a multi-hour session overlaps this interval (e.g., 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM), shooting pauses during this hour and resumes at 1:00 PM to ensure you receive your complete allocated shooting duration.

Arrival & Lateness Policy

Because studio slots are booked consecutively throughout the day, punctuality is essential:

  • Recommended Arrival: Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled shoot time. This allows you adequate time to freshen up, review wardrobe choices, and receive brief pose guidance.
  • Late Arrival: If you arrive late, your session time will still end at the originally scheduled conclusion time. The studio cannot extend session duration at the expense of subsequent booked clients.
  • Over 30 Minutes Tardy: Clients arriving more than 30 minutes past their booked time without advance phone/SMS notice will be classified as a No-Show, and their slot and downpayment will be forfeited.

Photo Retouching & Delivery Timelines

All photographs captured during your session undergo our mindful selection and calibration workflow:

  • Proofing Gallery: Low-resolution preview proofs or selection links are uploaded to your Client Portal within 3 to 5 business days following your session.
  • Final Retouched Master Files: Professional color grading, retouching, and export in full high-resolution JPEG format are completed within 7 to 10 business days after you finalize your photo selections.
  • Rush Processing: Express 24–48 hour turnaround is available as an optional add-on package, subject to existing studio lab queue volume.
  • Cloud Gallery Retention: Your completed digital files are hosted in your SnailShutter client portal for a minimum of 60 days from the date of upload. Clients are strongly urged to download and store backup copies on personal cloud/hard drives. SnailShutter is not liable for data loss once the 60-day hosting window expires.
  • RAW Files Policy: SnailShutter does not release unedited RAW camera files under standard consumer packages. RAW files represent unfinished artist negatives; only fully calibrated and edited images are released.
